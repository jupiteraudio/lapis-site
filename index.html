Free to use, no account

Open it and start. Free for students, teachers, researchers, charities, public bodies and hobbyists. Your shows are stored in your own browser, and you can save and open show files.

Science you can check

PA design, hearing, STI and sub arrays are calculated from published methods, with the sources named.

Hardware stays local

The Lapis Bridge runs on your computer and talks to your desks and devices. The website never touches your network.

How the bridge fits

Documentation and design work in the browser alone. To read or set things on a mixing desk, scan the network or send cues, download the bridge, start it, and Lapis in your browser pairs with it using a code shown in the bridge window. Every write to hardware asks you to confirm.

Works best in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. Safari blocks a web page from reaching a program on your own computer: with Safari, open Lapis from the bridge itself (it serves the same app at http://127.0.0.1:52400/).
